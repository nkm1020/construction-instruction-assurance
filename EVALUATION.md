# Evaluation Plan

## Primary operational metrics

- Correct target, object, and zone identification
- Dangerous-ambiguity detection recall and precision
- False-confirmation rate
- Worker read-back completion and semantic match
- Foreman clarification minutes per shift
- Bilingual support burden
- Time from instruction to confirmed understanding
- Instruction-sensitive rework or correction events

## Adoption metrics

- Weekly active supervisors and workers
- Instructions captured per active shift
- Percentage of changed instructions using the workflow
- Repeat use after the pilot novelty period
- Drop-off caused by latency or interaction friction

## Pilot comparison

Compare the proposed workflow against the site's actual baseline, such as radio, KakaoTalk, paper, or a bilingual foreman. Use matched crews or time blocks where feasible and record workload and instruction complexity.

## Critical test cases

- Ambiguous pronoun or location reference
- Two similar drawing objects or work zones
- Superseded instruction still visible in chat history
- Safety-material change
- Stale schedule or material state
- Noisy audio and code-switching
- Offline or high-latency conditions
- Worker acknowledgement of the wrong instruction version

## Commercial learning gates

A pilot should not graduate based on demos alone. Require recurring active use, measurable clarification burden, acceptable latency, strong dangerous-ambiguity detection, and evidence that existing workarounds are insufficient.

## Claim boundaries

Do not use accident reduction as an early ROI claim. Do not infer comprehension from attendance or signatures. Do not claim improved hiring or labor supply without a separate matched placement study.
