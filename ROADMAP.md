# Roadmap

## Phase 0: Field discovery

- Select one rebar or formwork workflow
- Observe instruction changes, clarification time, and current KakaoTalk/radio workarounds
- Define disqualification gates for sites with too little recurring pain
- Review privacy, recording, labor, and safety requirements

## Phase 1: Wizard-of-Oz prototype

- Push-to-talk capture
- Manual context entry and structured instruction form
- Two language pairs
- Worker read-back and version history
- Human-controlled clarification policy

## Phase 2: Context-grounded MVP

- Connect crew, zone, work package, drawing/BIM, material, and schedule context
- Add source display and timestamp/expiry
- Implement required-field and risk-based ambiguity rules
- Export an instruction audit record

## Phase 3: Eight-week paid pilot

- Target one 6-25 worker multilingual specialty crew
- Measure recurring use and direct operational outcomes
- Review false confirmations and near-miss ambiguity cases weekly
- Stop or redesign if adoption and clarification-time thresholds are not met

## Phase 4: Productization

- Change Assurance as the first paid workflow
- Day-0 multilingual onboarding as an adjacent module
- Integrations with existing messaging, BIM, and field-management systems
- Offline degradation and device-management hardening

## Phase 5: Bounded pre-hire study

Test whether trade-specific instruction comprehension can reduce employer uncertainty using a matched placement study. Treat this as a separate hypothesis, not a safety certification or proven expansion of labor supply.
